Reaching for the Stars

追逐星辰 · 我们能进入多远的太空？

读前热身 语篇精读 课堂检测 写作迁移
读前热身 教材 1a / 1b · 预测文本类型

1a · 你在哪里了解太空？

Where do you prefer to find information about space exploration? Choose the ones that are true for you.

可多选。请一位同学用 I prefer … because … 分享理由。

1b · 你会在哪里读到这篇文章？

Read the title and the text quickly. Where would you find an article like this?

判断文本类型。快速扫标题、专有名词和数字，想一想作者在讲故事、解释科学问题，还是报道一件新事件。

参考答案 · A popular science magazine. 文章围绕一个科学问题，用旅行者 1 号和距离数据解释现状、挑战与未来设想。
READING

语篇精读

Since the beginning of human history, people have looked up at the stars and wondered what they were like. Many ancient cultures believed that these points of light were from gods or other mysterious worlds. However, as technology and science developed, we learnt the truth about stars. They are real places like our own sun, and they are farther away than anyone imagined. This raises the question: Can we visit the stars one day?

自人类历史之初，人们就仰望星空，想知道星辰是什么样的。许多古代文明相信，这些光点来自神明或其他神秘世界。然而，随着科技的发展，我们逐渐了解了星辰的真相。它们像我们的太阳一样真实存在，也比任何人想象得更遥远。这就引出了一个问题：有朝一日，我们能造访星辰吗？

The man-made object which has travelled farthest from Earth is Voyager 1. This spacecraft was launched in 1977. In case Voyager 1 is found by alien life, a special record with sounds and pictures from Earth is carried on board. It shows who we are and where we are from. By 2025, Voyager 1 was over 25 billion kilometres from Earth, but that is still a long way from Earth’s second-closest star, Proxima Centauri. That star is 4.2 light years away—about 40 trillion kilometres!

飞得离地球最远的人造物体是旅行者 1 号。它于 1977 年发射。万一它被外星生命发现，飞船上带有一张特别的唱片，收录了来自地球的声音和图像，展示我们是谁、来自哪里。到 2025 年，旅行者 1 号已飞到离地球超过 250 亿千米的地方，但这一距离与地球第二近的恒星比邻星相比，仍相差很远。那颗恒星离我们有 4.2 光年，约 40 万亿千米！

The challenge of visiting the stars gets even bigger for space flights with humans. The farthest that any human has travelled in space is only about 400,000 kilometres, to the far side of the moon. Time is a big problem. At present, even a journey to the planet Mars will take around six to nine months. What will the astronauts eat and drink during this period? And how about their health and safety? These problems grow when we talk about visiting another star. It might take hundreds of thousands of years to get there!

对载人太空飞行而言，造访星辰的挑战更大。人类在太空中到过的最远处是月球背面，距离只有大约 40 万千米。时间是一大难题。以目前的条件，即使前往火星也需要约六到九个月。这段时间里，宇航员吃什么、喝什么？健康和安全又如何保障？说到造访另一颗恒星，这些问题就更严峻了。到那里可能要几十万年！

Scientists, however, believe that visiting the stars is not a completely wild dream. One idea is to have the astronauts sleep during their journey, like some animals do in winter. Another is to develop much faster spacecraft. While this is not possible right now, with nations and scientists cooperating with one another, there is hope that the necessary technology will be developed. Humans have photographed other galaxies, set foot on the moon, and even successfully landed rovers on Mars. It seems likely that one day we will reach the stars.

然而，科学家认为，造访星辰并非全然不切实际的梦想。一个设想是让宇航员在旅途中像有些动物过冬那样进入休眠。另一个设想是研制速度更快的航天器。虽然目前还做不到，但随着各国和科学家携手合作，人们有望研发出所需的技术。人类已经拍摄过其他星系、踏上过月球，甚至成功让探测车在火星着陆。看来有朝一日，我们很可能抵达星辰。

逐段讲解

第 1 段 / 共 4 段

从好奇到核心问题

找到 However 前后的变化。前面写古人怎样解释星光，后面写科学如何改变人们的认识。

… people have looked up at the stars and wondered what they were like.
have looked up … and (have) wondered …
and 连接两个并列谓语，第二个 have 省略。what they were like 是 wondered 的宾语从句，they 指星星。
raise a question
提出或引出一个问题。

What question does the writer raise?

Can we visit the stars one day?

用例子和数字说明距离

圈出 1977、over 25 billion kilometres 和 4.2 light years，分别找出它们说明什么。

in case … / on board
“以防万一”；“在飞船、船或飞机上”。was launched / is carried 都是被动语态。
billion / trillion / light year
十亿 / 万亿 / 光年。光年是光在一年内走过的距离。

Why does Voyager 1 carry a special record?

In case alien life finds Voyager 1, the record can show who we are and where we are from.

从距离读到生存难题

作者先用登月和火星旅程建立参照，再用两个问句把注意力转到宇航员身上。

at present / hundreds of thousands of
目前 / 数十万。
How about their health and safety?
How about …? 在这里用于追问：“他们的健康和安全又怎么办？”

Besides time, what problems does the writer mention?

Food and drink, and the astronauts’ health and safety.

从现实困难过渡到未来设想

One idea … Another … 引出两个设想。however 把语气从困难转向希望。

have the astronauts sleep
have + 宾语 + 动词原形 表示让某人做某事。
While …, with …, there is hope that …
While 引导让步状语从句；with + 名词短语 + cooperating 作状语；that 从句说明希望的具体内容。

What two ideas do scientists have?

Have astronauts sleep during the journey and develop much faster spacecraft.

课堂检测

课件补充 · 先选，再展示参考答案

1 · 识别文体

What kind of text is this?

B · 作者用实例和数据解释星际旅行。A 的线索应是虚构人物或情节，C 的线索应是某件新近事件。

2 · 理解数字的作用

Why does the writer give the distance to Proxima Centauri?

C · 第 2 段用 but 引出距离对比。A 把参照物理解成了飞行目的地，B 是文中未介绍的发射过程。

3 · 推断作者态度

How does the writer feel about visiting the stars one day?

A · there is hope 与 It seems likely 表示乐观。not possible right now 保留现实限制，排除 B 和 C。
WRITING

写作迁移

学写科普短段落

  1. 引出问题第 1 段 · 从好奇写起
  2. 说明现状第 2 段 · 举实例、列数据
  3. 分析困难第 3 段 · 说明问题为何棘手
  4. 提出设想第 4 段 · 给出可能的方向

Could humans live on Mars one day?

用 4–5 句话写一段说明文。引用课文里一个数字，解释一个困难，再用 might 提出一个可能的解决办法。

参考表达
Could humans live on Mars one day? At present, a trip to Mars takes around six to nine months. Astronauts would need enough food and water for the long journey. Growing some food on Mars might help people live there for longer.
